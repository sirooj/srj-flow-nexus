SPEC L362: ## 10. Permission vocabulary
SPEC L363: 
SPEC L364: Three of this document's rules turn on the difference between a requirement, a permission, and a prohibition, and one earlier revision conflated the second with the third. The distinction is stated once here and applies throughout.
SPEC L365: 
SPEC L366: | Phrasing | Implementation | Rejects when |
SPEC L367: |---|---|---|
SPEC L368: | **Required** / must | test the condition, reject on failure | the condition is absent |
SPEC L369: | **Permitted** / not required / need not | **accept either way**; the condition is informational at most | never — a permission cannot cause a rejection |
SPEC L370: | **Prohibited** / must not | test the condition, reject on presence | the condition is present |
SPEC L371: 
SPEC L372: **A permission is not a prohibition.** "The opposing candle need not touch the XOB" means a touching candle and a non-touching candle are both admissible. It does **not** mean a touch disqualifies, and no ruling in this document creates such a prohibition.
