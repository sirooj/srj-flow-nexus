```text

R-30  A FORM B IS ISSUED IN ONE EMISSION OR IT IS NOT ISSUED.

      A Form B is issuable only as a single continuous council emission in one session.
      Multiple PARTS are permitted under the output-budget rule, on three conditions:
      the part count and block-to-part map are stated in part 1's header; each part but
      the last ends in a continuation token; and NO part revises, strikes, corrects or
      supersedes any earlier part.

      If council finds a fault in an already-emitted part, the form is ABANDONED at
      that revision and reissued whole at the next revision number. There is no
      addendum, no strike and no erratum to a Form B. The revision number is cheap;
      an assembled form is not.

      CONSEQUENCE: v4 PART 2 as emitted is ABANDONED. It is superseded whole by
      TASK_155-v4-PART2-R1, which carries the corrected figures at authorship. Nobody
      applies a strike. Nobody merges anything.

Last issued R-30.
```

```text
★ ROLE-ADDRESSING RULE   - every instruction in a council document is addressed to a
  named role that holds the authority to perform it. An instruction to the operator may
  require only relay of a named artifact, or a statement of discretionary strategy
  meaning. Council never asks the operator to edit, verify, assemble, count, hash,
  select a branch or direct another role. A council document containing such an
  instruction is defective on that ground alone. Defect 152.
```

```text

R-32  A FORM B PART IS EMITTABLE ONLY IF IT CAN BE ISSUED TO THE BUILDER WITHOUT
      RENDERING, INTERPRETATION OR AUTHORSHIP ASSEMBLY.

      If a part contains a strike correction, the corrected text must be printed in the
      issued artifact as insertion-ready literal text, reviewed at authorship and
      verified by role. If it contains a delta-based edit, the AFTER block must be
      printed as complete insertion-ready literal text, reviewed at authorship and
      verified by role.

      The scribe may perform the mechanical work of text rendering into printable form
      before the author reviews it, but the author MUST verify the rendered block in its
      final emission form before the council issues it. The count statement must refer
      to that final printed literal text, not to a description the builder is expected
      to execute.

      CONSEQUENCE: v4 PART 2 R-1 and R-2, as presently constituted, are NOT emittable
      under this ruling because the AFTER blocks in EDIT 3 and EDIT 13 are encoded as
      diffs requiring builder interpretation to reach insertion text. The author must
      re-render both AFTER blocks as complete literal code and verify them before the
      part can be issued.

Last issued R-32.
```
