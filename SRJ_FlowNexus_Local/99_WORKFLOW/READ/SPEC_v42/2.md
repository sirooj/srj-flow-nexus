## 2. Three tests on one object

The twelve POI lines carry three distinct roles. They share only their price values.

| Role | Test | Evaluated | Scope |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Entry anchor retest** | wick penetrates the level by ≥1 point, body stays on the correct side | per bar | seeding a candidate, **entry only** |
| **Take-profit target** | level reached — a touch, no geometry | per bar, continuously | admission gate pre-entry; target selection post-entry |
| **POI-side exit** | body closes through the level | per bar | pre-confirmation invalidation; post-entry exit |

**2.1 The wick test is entry-only.** No role in exits, none in target evaluation. v3's silence here is what let "retest" leak into the exit vocabulary.

**2.2 A take-profit POI exits on touch.** Identical treatment to session liquidity. No wick-in/body-out, no confirmation candle, no retest sequence.

**2.3 The anchor asymmetry is deliberate.** The anchor line is excluded from take-profit candidates (§3.7's "opposite side"). But under §5 the anchor sitting behind the trade is precisely the supporting POI whose body-break exits you. **In scope for the exit test, out of scope for targets.**

**2.4 All twelve lines carry all three roles.** No tier restriction, no VWAP/POC distinction. Only the Yearly pair has never been observed to produce a retest, which is a data-coverage fact and not a rule.
