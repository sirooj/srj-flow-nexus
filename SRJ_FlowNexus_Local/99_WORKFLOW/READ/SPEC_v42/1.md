## 1. Vocabulary

### 1.1 Two terms that must never touch

| Term | Meaning | Where it applies |
|---|---|---|
| **structural bias** | the bias engine's output — the HTF 4H/1H/15m aggregate, and the 5-minute LTF bias, driven by order-block invalidation counts | gates entry at Step 2; LTF ignored post-entry; HTF flip exits (§5.6) |
| **POI side** | which side of a POI line price sits on, evaluated per bar | §5 entirely |

Every exit rule in §5 is POI side. None of it is structural bias.

### 1.2 Relevance

**An order block is *relevant* when it has been promoted to XOB.** Not on validation, not on activation. An unpromoted order block is not a candidate zone.

XOBs project rightward indefinitely until invalidated. **FVGs do not project.**

### 1.3 Retired concepts

**"Gap" / "jump."** Descriptive language for the nature of POC and VWAP movement, never a mechanism. There is no gap test, no relocation threshold, no discontinuity flag anywhere in this specification. Targets follow the nearest valid POI as values move; that is all.

**"Break and retest" as a sequence.** Not the operator's model. **"Break" is not an event; it is a state** — which side of the line price is on. Nothing to detect, no retest leg to wait for.

**"Current, freshest structure" as a qualifier on XOB in-play.** Withdrawn — see §3.5. It survives on the POI retest and the mean-reversion sweep.
