## 5. Exit and invalidation

### 5.1 The rule

One rule, one side test, evaluated **per bar**:

- **POI ahead** of the trade — above price for a LONG, below for a SHORT → take-profit target. **Exit on touch.**
- **POI behind** the trade → it supports the trade. **Exit only on a body close through it.** A wick through does nothing.

The body-close break is an **unconditional immediate exit**. Operator: *"i exit early or right there, disregarding if TP or SL."* Not weighed against a competing target.

### 5.2 Side is dynamic

Classification is per bar, not fixed at entry. A POI whose value moves may change side, and its role changes with it.

The case that produced the rule: a POC below a LONG relocates upward to sit just beneath the current candle. It has **not** become a target — still below price, still behind, still supporting — so it remains under the body-close rule. Price retraces, a body closes below it, side flips, exit. That is 8/17.

Contrast: a POC relocating to sit *above* a LONG is ahead from that bar forward and is a touch-exit target.

### 5.3 What this section does not contain

No gap concept (§1.3). **No favour/adverse asymmetry** — a POI moving toward the trade and away from it are treated identically; the rule is blind to direction of movement. No distance, size, or width. The tests are *which side* and *body versus wick*.

### 5.4 Pre-confirmation invalidation

A candidate armed and waiting for its confirming close is **dead** if the POI behind it is body-broken before that close. Operator's reasoning: it conflicts with the 1R admission requirement.

Two distinct pre-entry deaths, no overlap:

| Situation | Mechanism |
|---|---|
| POI relocates **ahead** and nearer than 1R | the 1R admission gate |
| POI **behind** is body-broken | the POI-side test |

Below or touching without a body close through → still valid, with the recorded caution that after the confirming entry a higher-tier POI may overtake the candle and force an early exit.

### 5.5 Pending entry lifecycle

Entry is a **limit at the confirming candle's close**. Price running away moves nothing and cancels nothing. It **fills on a wick**, not a close. While pending it cancels on a bias flip, or on the three-flag conjunction — which is the same event. **Staleness and structural renewal do not cancel.** If the filling candle's own close invalidates the setup, exit immediately rather than waiting for a subsequent close.

### 5.6 Structural bias, post-entry

| Timeframe | Post-entry structural flip |
|---|---|
| LTF (5-minute) | **ignored** — the trade is held |
| HTF (4H/1H/15m aggregate) | **exit** at the flipping HTF candle's confirmation close |

The HTF rule is default, scoped to trend-following setups, and flagged as a toggle for future comparison — a mean-reversion setup was never justified by HTF agreement, so an HTF flip is not an argument against it. **No supporting data; recorded as ruled, not measured.**
