## 4. Evaluation timing

**Default: evaluate at the next candle's open**, at three sites — the POI retest that seeds a candidate, the confirmation candle, and the exit.

Rationale: a retest valid at the breaking candle's close can be invalid at the next open, and in that case the setup is invalid. The 8/17 exit was already this compound test — a body close through the POC, measured against the POI value as it stood at the **next** open.

**Toggle, for future comparison.** Must be a compile-time constant, not a user input: indicator handle bindings are positional and one fails outright at a fourth parameter.

**Measurement cost, stated because it is real.** Defaulting this on changes admission at three sites at once. The resulting baseline is comparable to no prior baseline, and the three sites cannot be separated from one another. Recommended mitigation: build the reads as diagnostics first, logging verdicts and changing no control flow, so the population is known before the rule governs anything.
