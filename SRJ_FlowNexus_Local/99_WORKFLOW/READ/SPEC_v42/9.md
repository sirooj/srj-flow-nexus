## 9. What is not known

**9.1 Feed divergence bounds every external comparison.** The operator reads structure on TradingView/OANDA; the system runs a Dukascopy demo feed. His own screenshot shows Dukascopy at `4H Bear, 1H Bear, 15m Bear` on a day his OANDA read said the trend-following setup was long. **No edit can close this.** Any bias-shaped or divergence-shaped disagreement must exclude feed divergence before it is attributed to logic.

**9.2 Realised gains are not attributable to this entry pipeline.** Every journalled gain is entry plus exit discipline, and only the entry is built. Recall and precision — matched trades over journalled trades, matched trades over emitted signals — remain valid. **Gain magnitudes do not.**

**9.3 The relevance rule is currently unobservable.** §3.5.1's two known instances, one on a rejected session and one on an untraded zone, are indistinguishable from the orchestrator's position. Until the promotion bar is exported the rule cannot be implemented **or verified**.

**9.4 Miss attribution is by proximate death, not by cause.** A candidate can carry several pending deaths; removing the one that fires first reveals the next rather than saving the setup. Demonstrated: retiring the across-time tier replacement freed one named miss's candidate, which then died on structural invalidation fifteen minutes later, and that session's four attempts split across two entirely different open items. **Every recall claim attached to a miss table is an upper bound.**

**9.5 Sample size.** Twelve journalled trades against ten emitted signals. Recall 1/12, precision 1/10, **structural agreement 0/12** (§9.7). The operator's own acceptance rate is roughly 12 in 240 candidates. **No gate may be tuned on this sample.**

**9.6 One replay-order defect is confirmed in the port and unmeasured.** Two different order blocks replayed onto a single discovery bar can each increment a per-bar invalidation counter and trip a bias flip in one step. Bounded to one bar, but one bar is sufficient. Attaches an unquantified doubt to every bias-misalignment death. Absent from the original Pine implementation, which has no replay mechanism.

**9.7 The only reproduced trade is not a structural match.** The single journalled trade the system has reproduced was entered on an XOB spanning **1.15805–1.15843**, formed at 05:30 and projected rightward, with the stop at a swing low of **1.15835 lying inside that zone**, and the entry at **1.15944 — 101 points above the zone's high.** The zone was in play by swing penetration, not by price retracing into it.

The orchestrator held **1.15784–1.15796** for four bars, judged it correctly out of play, then adopted **1.15870–1.15959** on its arming bar and signalled on that. **Neither orchestrator zone overlaps the traded one.** The stop references differ by 35 points and are not the same kind of object (§3.7). The entry *prices* coincide because the operator's entry falls inside the zone the orchestrator adopted — coincidence, not agreement.

The match is on session, direction, anchor POI and approximate timing. **The entry pipeline has never been shown to match an operator entry on the same retracement structure. Recall of 1/12 overstates agreement; structural agreement is 0/12.**

**9.8 Protocol gates protecting the control are provisional.** Several measurement protocols treat "the 08.17 signal must survive" as project-stopping. Per §9.7 that signal is not a structural match, so the gate protects a coincidence. Retain it as a **change detector** — its disappearance still indicates that an edit altered admission — but it is not a correctness requirement.

**9.9 Age is not a disqualifier.** XOBs project rightward indefinitely until invalidated. The one verified operator zone was **ten hours old** at entry. Any recency bound on zone selection contradicts §3.5 directly and would have removed the only reproduced trade's structure. A promotion-bar bound (§8) is compatible with this; an age bound is not.

**9.10 The in-play window, not the selector, is the binding constraint.** The current in-play test consults the evaluation bar's range, then the most recent confirmed protective-side swing, then the next distinct one — **two swings, nothing further back.** The one verified operator zone was in play via a swing roughly eighty bars earlier. **Even handed the correct zone, the orchestrator would have reported it out of play on every bar.** Diagnosis of adopt-and-inherit as a selector defect (§3.5.1) is therefore secondary; the primary defect is upstream of it and needs no new export to fix. The test exists in three copies, which must be reconciled together or not at all.

**9.11 The two-swing depth has no ruling behind it.** It was chosen to mirror §3.7's two-swing stop branch, on the reasoning that two fixes the system's structural depth. §3.5 now states there is no bar-count limit, so the correct bound is the structural leg, not a swing count. Whether *every* confirmed swing in the leg should be tested, or some other structural bound applies, is not ruled.

**9.12 The non-touching XOB opposing candle has no observed instance.** §3.6 permits it by ruling and the current build rejects it, so no data exists on how often it would arise. Its population is unknown until the overlap requirement is dropped.
